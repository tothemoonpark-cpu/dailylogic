Privacy Policy

Daily Logic, by Daily Logic Studio
Effective date: 9 October 2026

Summary.

  • You do not need an account, and we (the developer) do not collect your personal data.
  • Your puzzle progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob.
  • Google may collect device identifiers, including your advertising ID, an approximate location based on your IP address, and usage data, to show and measure ads.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Android app "Daily Logic" (the "app"), published by Daily Logic Studio ("we", "us").

Contact: tothemoon_park@naver.com

2. Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.

The app has no account, no sign-up and no server of our own.

We do not receive your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

3. Data stored on your device

The app saves your settings and progress on your device only.

This includes your chosen language, solved puzzles, your streak, unlocked past puzzles and whether you turned on the reminder.

This data never leaves your device through us.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland).

The app shows banner ads, full-screen ads at natural breaks between puzzles, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a hint or to open a past puzzle.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device, such as model, operating system and language;
  • usage data about ads, such as which ads were shown or tapped.

Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Consent for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads are used.

You can accept or decline, and the app works either way.

If you decline, Google may still show non-personalized ads, which use more limited data.

To change your choice later, clear the app's data or reinstall the app, and the consent form will appear again.

6. Fonts

The app may load its fonts from Google Fonts.

When it does, your device connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.

If there is no connection, the app uses the fonts already on your device.

Google Fonts privacy information: https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy

7. Notifications

The app can send an optional daily reminder that new puzzles are ready.

The app asks for your permission first, and the reminder is created and shown only on your device.

You can turn it off at any time in your system settings (Settings > Apps > Daily Logic > Notifications).

8. Sharing results

When you tap "Share result", the app opens your device's share menu with a short text of your result.

Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Security and retention

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not keep any on our side.

Data held by Google is kept according to Google's own policies linked above.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing.

For data processed by Google for ads, use the controls and links above or contact Google.

For any question about this app, email us at tothemoon_park@naver.com.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
